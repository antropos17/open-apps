## Why it's listed

Device Hub Pro is a SwiftUI app that shows Android emulators, Android
phones (USB or Wi-Fi, with QR pairing) and iOS simulators in one window,
plus physical iPhones. Each device is mirrored live and takes touch and
keyboard input.

A Controls panel changes network, battery, location, appearance and
permissions, and sends deep links and push notifications. Live logs
(logcat and simulator logs), screenshots, screen recordings, app install
and file transfer sit next to the mirror. It also shows the iPhone Duo
foldable simulator in 3D with its fold postures.

MIT licensed. Download it from the releases page. Needs macOS 26 or later.
